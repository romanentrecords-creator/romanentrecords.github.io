ABOUT ROMAN ENT RECORDS

Music without one lane.

Roman Ent Records is an independent, multi-genre music label built around music with character, atmosphere and story. Our releases combine original creative direction, songwriting concepts and production with AI-assisted tools to explore artists and musical worlds across metal, country, rock, blues, dance and cinematic music.

The label is also home to The Transistor Radio Show, where music, characters and story-driven broadcasting come together.

Human-directed creativity. AI-assisted production. Music built around the story.
